## About

I am a systems-focused engineer from **Mirpur Khas, Sindh, Pakistan**, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, distributed software, and social enterprise.

My primary areas of work include:

- Agentic AI workflows and tool-using systems
- Model Context Protocol integrations
- GraphRAG and retrieval-based AI architectures
- Backend APIs, microservices, and asynchronous workflows
- Community platforms designed for low-resource environments

I founded **Hunarmand**, a labor-matching initiative that has connected more than **1,500 local workers** with employment opportunities.

I also manage technology and operations at **Al-Ahmed Bakery**, where I converted paper-based processes into digital inventory and operational workflows that contributed to **1.5× revenue growth**.
